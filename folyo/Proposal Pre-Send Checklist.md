# Proposal Pre-Send Checklist

Use this after you've written your proposal, before you hit send.

## Cover Letter / Introduction

☐ Opens with their problem, not your credentials

☐ Makes clear I understand why they're doing this project

☐ Feels specific to them, not like a template

## The Offer

☐ Line items are contextualized within benefits (not just tasks + prices)

☐ Price is tied to a specific outcome or metric they care about

☐ Scope reflects what they actually need, not just what they asked for

## Credibility

☐ Includes a "why us" section with specific proof (clients, results, awards, experience)

☐ Case studies are relevant to this type of project

☐ Case studies are skimmable — key metrics/results are visually called out

## Risk Reduction

☐ Shows we're low risk to hire (clear process, relevant experience, references)

☐ Addresses likely objections (price, timeline, "why not someone cheaper")

☐ Timeline is realistic and clearly laid out

## The Skim Test

☐ If they only read the headings and bolded text, they'd still get the main points

☐ Nothing critical is buried in long paragraphs

## Before You Send

☐ I've already reached out (LinkedIn, email, phone, Q&A window)

☐ I have a follow-up plan for after I submit

☐ I know who the decision maker is (or have done my best to find out)

## Score It

| Category | Points |
|---|---|
| Cover Letter | 2 points each (6 total) |
| The Offer | 3 points each (9 total) |
| Credibility | 2 points each (6 total) |
| Risk Reduction | 2 points each (6 total) |
| Skim Test | 1 point each (2 total) |
| Before You Send | 2 points each (6 total) |

**Your Score: ___ / 35**

| Score | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 30-35 | Strong proposal. Send it. |
| 22-29 | Solid, but revisit the gaps before sending. |
| 14-21 | Needs work. You're leaving points on the table. |
| Below 14 | Don't send this yet. Rework it. |

Remember: The proposal is important, but it's rarely what decides who wins. The relationship you build around it matters more.
