# RFP Evaluation Checklist

Use this before you decide to pursue an RFP. If you can't check most of these boxes, consider skipping it.

## Red Flags (If any of these, think twice)

☐ The RFP is NOT 60 pages of legalese and compliance requirements

☐ The scope and budget are realistic (not "complete redesign + app + SEO for $15K")

## Green Flags (The more of these, the better)

☐ Clear problem statement — they explain why they need this, not just what they want

☐ Named contact person (not just submissions@company.com)

☐ Q&A window available before the deadline

☐ Timeline, scope, and budget all make sense together

☐ They came through a referral or are already familiar with your work

## Practical Fit

☐ This is the kind of work we actually want to do

☐ We have relevant experience to reference

☐ I can put together a solid proposal in 24 hours, not 10

## Quick Gut Check

☐ If I win this, I'll be happy about it (not dreading the project)

## Score It

| Category | Points |
|---|---|
| Red Flags | 2 points each (for checking the box — meaning no red flag present) |
| Green Flags | 2 points each |
| Practical Fit | 2 points each |
| Gut Check | 3 points |

**Your Score: ___ / 23**

| Score | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 19-23 | Strong opportunity. Prioritize this one. |
| 13-18 | Worth pursuing if you have capacity. |
| 7-12 | Borderline. Proceed only if it's a slow week. |
| Below 7 | Skip it. Your time is better spent elsewhere. |

At 10 proposals per month, you can't afford to spend hours on long-shot RFPs. Use this score to prioritize.
